## Terms of Use

### 1. The service
YUNABI (the "Service") offers the residents of 結 -YUI- everyday Japanese lessons, a map of useful places and a calendar of events. It is operated by Juha Bak, an individual. Anyone can read it. Adding places or events and reporting items need no sign-up: an anonymous account is created automatically the first time you post.

### 2. Anonymous accounts
An anonymous account is tied to the browser you use. If you clear your browser data or change devices, you can no longer edit or delete what you posted before. If you then need something deleted, ask through the contact address below.

### 3. What you post
Places and events you post are public to every user. Do not post:
- anyone's home or room location, name, contact details or anything else that identifies a person, including your own
- advertising, spam, or the same thing over and over
- anything unlawful or against public order and morals, or that infringes someone else's rights (copyright, portrait rights, reputation and so on)
- information you know to be false

### 4. Rights in what you post
You keep the rights in what you post. You allow the operator to show it in the Service and to copy and store it as far as running the Service needs (including backups and restores).

### 5. Reports and the operator's actions
Users can report items. An item reported from three different accounts is hidden automatically and checked by the operator. The operator may hide or delete items that break these terms and may stop an account from posting.

### 6. Limits
To prevent floods of posts, the number of posts is limited per account and per connection (for example, 15 new places per account per day). If automated mass posting appears, a check (CAPTCHA) may be switched on.

### 7. Accuracy and liability
Places and events are posted by users; the operator does not guarantee they are accurate (opening hours, times, locations and so on). The Service is free and may change or stop without notice. Except in cases of intent or gross negligence, the operator is not liable for damage arising from use of the Service.

### 8. Changes
The operator may change these terms. Changed terms take effect when posted in the Service. Important changes are announced in the app in advance.

### 9. Governing law and jurisdiction
These terms are governed by the laws of Japan. The district court with jurisdiction over the operator's address has exclusive jurisdiction in the first instance over disputes about the Service.

## Privacy Policy

### 1. Operator
Juha Bak (individual). The operator's address is provided without delay on request.

### 2. What we collect

| What | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous account ID (a random value) | When you first post or report | Editing and deleting your own items, limits, handling reports |
| Places and events you post, their language, posting and edit dates | When you post or edit | Providing the Service (public to all users) |
| Automatic translations of place descriptions (into the other two of English, Japanese, Korean) | After a description is posted or edited | Showing it to users of other languages (public; the original is one tap away) |
| Reports (item, reason) | When you report | Automatic hiding and the operator's review |
| A record of posts (when, what) | When you post | Daily limits |
| A pseudonymised value of your IP address (a hash mixed with a secret that changes daily) | With each post | Preventing abuse |
| Records of the operator's actions | When the operator hides, deletes, restores or suspends | Record keeping and audit |

**We do not collect:** your name, email address or current location.

### 3. Location
Your current location is used only on your device, only after you allow it, to centre the map and estimate walking times. It is never sent to our server; the only coordinates the server receives are those of places you post. When the map loads its images (tiles), the tile numbers of the area on screen go to OpenStreetMap's tile servers, narrowing the area as you zoom in.

### 4. Automatic translation
After a place description is posted or edited, it is translated into the other two languages by Microsoft's machine translation (Azure AI Translator), stored, and shown in each viewer's screen language (marked "Translated automatically", with the original available). Only the description's text and language are sent to Microsoft — not the place name, location, account or anything about who posted it. Microsoft states that it does not store text sent for text translation.

### 5. Analytics
To understand how the app is used, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies and counts only the screens viewed, approximate country, browser and device type, and loading speed; it does not identify individuals or track them across sites.

### 6. What stays on your device
Your language setting, the cards you collected and whether you allowed location are kept only in your browser's storage (localStorage) and are not sent to the server. Clearing your browser data removes them.

### 7. Access logs
Servers of the companies that provide the Service may keep access records (IP address, time, browser information and so on). In particular, the account service (Supabase Auth) records the IP address as is. These are kept according to each company's policy.

### 8. Verification (CAPTCHA)
If there is an automated attack, the operator may switch on a Cloudflare Turnstile check. While it is on, Cloudflare processes browser and device signals at your first post to tell people from programs. It is normally off.

### 9. How long we keep it

| What | Kept |
|---|---|
| Places and events | Until deleted. Deleted items are erased after 30 days; past events after one year |
| Anonymous accounts | Deleted one year after the last post or report. Places stay after their account is deleted (without an owner; no longer editable) |
| Reports and records of the operator's actions | One year |
| Record of posts | 48 hours |
| Pseudonymised IP values | One hour for counting posts; erased from security records after 48 hours (the security records themselves are kept 90 days) |
| Automatic translations | Like the original: deleted and redone when the description changes, deleted when the place is deleted |
| Backups | Weekly, encrypted, kept 90 days. Backups do not contain the daily secret, so pseudonymised IP values cannot be reversed |

To run the Service, we entrust the handling of personal data to the following companies, all outside Japan.

| Company | What they do | Where the data is |
|---|---|---|

The operator entrusts this handling after confirming that these companies have systems meeting the standards set by Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information. For the personal information protection systems of each country, see the information provided by Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission.

Apart from the above, we do not give personal data to third parties except as required by law. There is no advertising. The only analytics is the cookie-free Cloudflare Web Analytics.

### 11. Security
Users' devices cannot read or write the database directly; writes go only through server-side functions that check them. Who posted an item is not shown to other users, and the operator sees only pseudonymous account IDs. Administrator sign-in uses a password and two-factor authentication. Backups are encrypted.

### 12. Requests to see, correct or delete
Because accounts are anonymous, the operator cannot confirm that a person asking is the owner of an account. So you edit or delete your own items yourself under "My posts" in the same browser. If you lost your device or do not use the app (for example, a shop owner, or someone whose home was posted), send the item's name and location through the contact address, and the operator will check and act on it.

### 13. Changes
Changes to this policy are posted in the Service.
